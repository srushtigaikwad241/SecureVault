Cyber Law & Compliance

Understand the legal responsibilities related to cybersecurity, data protection, electronic records and responsible handling of cyber incidents.

01

What is Cyber Law?

Cyber law refers to laws and legal principles that apply to computers, digital systems, electronic information, online activities and cyber offences.

Cybersecurity systems should not only protect information technically, but should also support responsible access, privacy, evidence handling and incident reporting.

02

Important Legal Frameworks

Some important Indian legal frameworks relevant to digital security and cyber activities include:

💻
LAW 01

Information Technology Act, 2000

The Information Technology Act provides an important legal framework for electronic records, electronic transactions and various computer-related offences in India.

LAW 02

Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

This framework addresses the processing and protection of digital personal data and responsibilities associated with personal information.

Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023

The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita is India's principal criminal law framework and may be relevant when digital activities are connected with criminal offences.

03

Responsible Cybersecurity Practices

Users and organizations should follow responsible practices when handling digital systems and information.

🔑

Authorized Access

Access systems and files only when you have appropriate authorization.

🛡️

Protect Sensitive Data

Use appropriate security controls to protect confidential and personal information.

📋

Maintain Records

Maintain appropriate audit and incident records to support accountability and investigation.

🚨

Report Incidents

Suspicious or unauthorized activity should be reported through the appropriate process.

05

Cybersecurity Compliance Checklist

Basic practices that should be considered when designing and operating a secure digital system.

✓

Authenticate Users

Verify users before allowing access to protected resources.

✓

Apply Access Control

Give users only the access they need.

✓

Protect Sensitive Information

Apply encryption and other suitable security controls to sensitive data.

✓

Maintain Audit Records

Record relevant security activities for monitoring and investigation.

✓

Preserve Data Integrity

Use integrity verification mechanisms to detect unexpected changes.

✓

Report Security Incidents

Follow appropriate organizational and legal reporting procedures when incidents occur.
